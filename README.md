# Bloxport

The Roblox account manager for Windows: run many accounts at once, Anti-AFK, auto-rejoin, launch groups, private servers, a built-in authenticator, limiteds, and sales and player stats for your communities and games.

## Download

Get **Bloxport Setup x.y.z.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Dragonmaster0973/bloxport/releases/latest) and run it. Windows may say it "protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Updates

Bloxport checks this page for new versions. When one is out, **Update to x.y.z** shows in the sidebar; press it and the app installs the new version and restarts with all your accounts and settings in place.

This repository only holds the installers. Your accounts, passwords and data never leave your PC.
